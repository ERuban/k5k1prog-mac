### What's fixed
- **No more `K5K1Prog.ini` in Applications.** Settings (port, speed, verbose level, window position) are now kept the macOS way, in the app's preferences. Your settings from 1.0 / 1.1 are moved over automatically and the old `K5K1Prog.ini` is deleted.
- **File dialogs start in Documents** instead of the Applications folder.
