### What's new
- **Automatic updates.** K5K1 Prog checks GitHub for new releases once a day and installs them with one click (**K5K1 Prog → Check for Updates…**). Updates are signed and verified before they are installed.
- **About window** (**K5K1 Prog → About K5K1 Prog**) with version, authors and license.
- **New app icon.**
- **UV-K5 V2 / UV-K6 V2 protection.** These radios (bootloader 1.xx, PY32F030 chip) run neither V1 nor V3/K1 firmware: flashing one leaves the radio with a blank screen. Firmware upload to them is now refused before anything is sent. Reading and writing configuration and calibration still work.

### Upgrading from 1.0.0
1.0.0 has no updater: download `K5K1-Prog-1.1.0.dmg` once and replace the app in **Applications**. From now on updates come by themselves.

**Back up your calibration (Read Calibration) before you flash anything.**
